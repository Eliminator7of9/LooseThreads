About

I'm Alex — a PPE graduate, currently doing a master's in Political Science, and someone who thinks about people and life far more than is probably useful. This site is where that thinking gets written down instead of just circling in my head.

I don't write this because I've worked things out. Most of it is the opposite — honest reflection while I'm still mid-figure-out, on career, on friendship, on what it means to feel behind or ahead of the people around you. I've come to think that being candid about not knowing is its own kind of useful, both to write and to read.

"I'd rather write the honest, unfinished version than the tidy one I'll believe in six months."

Outside of writing, my interests run wide — law, insurance, journalism, parliamentary politics, and a long-standing pull toward the Middle East and international affairs. I'm a generalist by instinct, which is either my biggest asset or the thing standing between me and a clear niche. This site is partly how I'm working that out too.

If something here resonates, or you just want to say hello, get in touch.

Written slowly, published honestly.
